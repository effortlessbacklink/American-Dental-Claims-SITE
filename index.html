American Dental Claims

About

American Dental Claims helps dental practices across the United States simplify billing and revenue cycle management. Based in Edmond, Oklahoma, the company provides support with claim submission, insurance verification, payment posting, patient statements, reporting, and more.

Every practice works with a dedicated account manager who understands its payers, history, and billing needs. Learn more about their services at American Dental Claims.

The goal is to help dental practices get claims paid correctly the first time and keep their ledgers clean. By handling time-consuming billing tasks, American Dental Claims helps front desk teams spend more time focused on patients.

FAQ

What dental billing services does American Dental Claims provide?

Services include claim submission and tracking, insurance verification, payment posting, ledger audits, patient statements, reporting, staff training, and fee schedule management.

Will my practice have a dedicated account manager?

Yes. Each practice works with one dedicated account manager who understands its billing history and payer requirements.

How can American Dental Claims help my dental practice?

They help reduce billing workload, improve claim processing, and keep patient ledgers organized so your staff can focus more on patient care.
